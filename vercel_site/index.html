Quantum Autoencoder vs PCA for Compression of Downsampled MNIST Digits

A simulator-based comparison · Qiskit, Qiskit Aer, NumPy · by Aditya Rao

A 4×4 MNIST digit has 16 pixels, which are amplitude-encoded into 4 qubits. A trainable quantum autoencoder compresses them to 1, 2 or 3 latent qubits and is compared with PCA at matched size (2, 4, 8 components).

Key finding. A post-selected quantum autoencoder can at best match PCA without a mean image (Ky Fan’s theorem), and with enough layers it does. No quantum advantage is claimed: PCA is faster and at least as accurate.

About

Means over 5 stratified train/test splits (L=12 on 3 splits). Real MNIST subset of 5,000 images; exact classical simulation of 4-qubit circuits, validated against Qiskit with 26 tests. Full report, code and notebook are in the project files.
